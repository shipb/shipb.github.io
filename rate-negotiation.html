The Problem

Your Clients Want Receipts, Not Promises

You renegotiated their UPS contract. You know you saved them money. But a handshake isn't enough — they want to see the numbers.

Manually pulling invoices and building spreadsheets takes hours. It's easy to miss surcharges, accessorials, or zone-level differences. And if you can't show clear savings, it's harder to justify your fee.

ShipBoss does the math for you. Upload the old and new rate cards, and we generate a side-by-side comparison — line by line, service by service. Then we benchmark it against FedEx, DHL, Amazon Shipping, and other carriers so you can show the full picture.

How It Works

Four Steps. Full Transparency.

From invoice upload to savings proof in minutes.

1

Upload the Invoice

Your client uploads the customer's current carrier invoice into ShipBoss. This invoice is the ground truth for every line item.

2

Re-Quote Line by Line

ShipBoss takes each shipment from the invoice and re-quotes it to determine what would have been paid under the old rates.

3

Compare Against Old Rates

We compare the current invoice against the previously negotiated rates stored in the system, showing the exact savings per shipment.

4

Invoice the Customer

With the savings calculated, generate a branded invoice showing the customer exactly what you saved them — and what they owe for your services.
