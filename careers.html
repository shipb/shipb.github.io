Customer Service & Technical Support Specialist

Jerusalem, Israel Hybrid position Monday–Thursday

Corporate Freight Savers (CFS) & ShipBoss are looking for a friendly, responsible, and tech-savvy individual to join our growing team! We’re seeking someone with excellent communication skills, strong problem-solving abilities, and a passion for helping customers.

Working Hours | Monday–Thursday

3:00 PM – 8:00 PM: In our Jerusalem office

8:00 PM – 12:00 AM: Flexible work-from-home availability for customer support as needed

Evening support is typically light, with the flexibility to handle customer issues as they arise.

What You’ll Do

  • Assist US-based customers with shipping and logistics inquiries.
  • Provide customer and technical support for the ShipBoss platform.
  • Troubleshoot shipping, billing, and system-related issues.
  • Communicate with customers via phone, email, and live chat.
  • Work alongside our Israel and US support teams to ensure excellent customer service.

What We’re Looking For

  • Excellent English, both spoken and written.
  • Strong computer skills and technical aptitude.
  • Friendly, patient, and professional communication.
  • Excellent organizational and problem-solving skills.
  • Dependable, proactive, and able to work independently.
  • Previous experience in customer service, logistics, or technical support is a plus, but not required.

What We Offer

  • Hybrid work arrangement
  • Supportive international team
  • Full training provided
  • Opportunity for professional growth
  • Competitive compensation based on experience
