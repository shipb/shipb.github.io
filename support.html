FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my carrier account?

Go to Settings → Carriers in your ShipBoss dashboard. Enter your carrier account credentials and ShipBoss will pull in your negotiated rates automatically.

Which carriers does ShipBoss support?

ShipBoss integrates with 50+ carriers including FedEx, UPS, USPS, DHL, and many regional carriers. See the full list on our Carrier Integration page.

Can I import orders from my Shopify or WooCommerce store?

Yes. SyncNShip connects directly to Shopify, WooCommerce, and Magento. Orders auto-import the moment a customer checks out. Learn more on the E-commerce Integration page.

What plans are available?

ShipBoss offers a Free plan, Essential plan ($9.99/mo), and Pro plan ($24.99/mo). View full details on the Pricing page.
