FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a credit card to sign up?

No. You can sign up for the Free plan and start creating labels immediately without entering any payment information.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan at any time?

Yes. You can change your plan at any time from your account settings. Changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

What carriers are supported?

ShipBoss integrates with over 50 carriers including FedEx, UPS, USPS, and many regional carriers. New integrations are added regularly.

Is e-commerce integration available on all plans?

SyncNShip e-commerce integration (Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento) is available on the Pro plan. Contact us for enterprise options.

Still have questions?

Our team is happy to walk you through the platform and help you pick the right plan.
